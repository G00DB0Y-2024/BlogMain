##### Comment 1: 摘要缺乏卫星特色与离线阶段强调

- 意见回顾：摘要方法论过多，未突出卫星路由特点；未强调离线方法的作用阶段（地面互联网也适用，不够凸显特色）。

- 修改方案: 新摘要

  LEO卫星星座以其强大的数据传输能力正在成为Non-Terrestrial Networks的有效补充, 而路由优化则是保证其服务效能的关键. LEO卫星星座路由离线优化阶段利用地面站遥测日志进行, 基于已有策略的数据进行批量训练以迭代更新, 并安全验证后再上注部署, 以减缓星载算力压力以及在线探索的试错成本. 然而离线训练尤其在动作分布支撑集小的低秩数据集中常面临高方差和外推误差问题, 传统正则化约束或是模仿学习难以使agent有效优化策略, 且在设备性能差异和邻居作用的因果混淆下难以有效利用数据并和在线场景对齐. This paper proposes an offline reinforcement framework Counterfactual Policy Boosting (CFPB)  based on Counterfactual Inference使离线智能体能够 explore potential better paths and achieve the optimal offline strategy by minimizing this trajectory regret 以提高数据利用率并识别因果效应. 实验结果表明，所提出的方法显著降低了路由离线评估方差，并且在路由性能方面优于基线方法。此外，可扩展性泛化实验和流量敏感性测试表明，该方法在合理范围内保持了稳健的优化性能和稳定的外推效果。

  > Low Earth Orbit (LEO) satellite constellations are emerging as a crucial component of Non-Terrestrial Networks (NTNs) and routing optimization is fundamental to ensuring service quality. Offline training and optimization are conducted prior to orbital upload to mitigate onboard computational limits and the high risks of online exploration. However, this phase frequently suffers from high variance and extrapolation errors. Compounded by inherent causal confusion from heterogeneous device performances and neighborhood interactions, traditional regularization constraints or imitation learning struggle to enable the agent to optimize policies effectively. This paper proposes an offline reinforcement learning framework Counterfactual Policy Boosting (CFPB) to improve data utilization efficiency. It enables the offline agent to explore potential better paths and achieve unbiased identification of action returns. Experimental results demonstrate that CFPB significantly reduces the offline routing evaluation variance and outperforms baseline methods in routing performance. Furthermore, adaptability analysis on incomplete constellation deployments and traffic sensitivity tests show that the method maintains robust optimization performance and stable extrapolation effects within a reasonable range.
