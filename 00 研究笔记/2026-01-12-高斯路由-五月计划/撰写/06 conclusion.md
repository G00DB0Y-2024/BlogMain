在这篇文章中, 提出了一种Ising-Heuristic Cooperative Reinforcement Learning, IHCRL的强化学习框架用于解决低轨卫星星座路由问题, 并基于意图向量驱动的可学习Ising Field实现了去中心化的多智能体的涌现协同, 促进了收敛以及对多智能体分布式决策情景下的局部最优问题. 实验表明IHCRL相较于传统方法显著降低了平均端到端时延和平均负载, 通过减少拥塞点以提升吞吐量, 同时提升了星座总体链路利用率, 对于动态流量注入情况也有较好的适应能力. 研究强调了Agentic AI的涌现协同工作模式在分布式路由问题上的潜力, 未来工作侧重于提高Ising模型和智能体的耦合程度, 以显式的方式实现自组织, 而非依靠外在自旋编码和策略扰动的范式.

This paper proposes an Ising-Heuristic Cooperative Reinforcement Learning (IHCRL) framework to address routing problems in Low Earth Orbit (LEO) satellite constellations. By leveraging an intent-vector-driven, learnable Ising Field, the approach achieves emergent cooperation in decentralized multi-agent systems, thereby facilitating convergence and mitigating local optimality issues in distributed decision-making scenarios. Experimental results demonstrate that IHCRL significantly reduces average E2E delay and mean load compared to conventional methods. Furthermore, by alleviating congestion nodes, the method enhances throughput and improves overall link usage within the constellation, while also exhibiting robust adaptability to dynamic traffic injection patterns. The study underscores the potential of Agentic AI's emergence of collaboration in addressing distributed routing challenges. Future work will focus on enhancing the coupling between the Ising model and the agents to achieve self-organization beyond the paradigm reliant on external spin encoding and policy perturbation.
