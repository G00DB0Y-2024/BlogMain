### Routing optimization for LEO satellite constellations based on Ising-Heuristic cooperative reinforcement learning



##### Abstract

为满足近地卫星星座通信网络对低延迟、节能、动态适应性的需求，本文提出了一种分布式路由架构, 提出一种伊辛模型启发的合作强化学习架构Ising-Heuristic Cooperative Reinforcement Learning, IHCRL 进行求解。在所提出的方法中，智能体主动感知环境并做出去中心化的路由决策以实现端到端时延和能耗的最小化以及链路负载均衡, 利用Dueling-DQN的价值函数生成局部冲激信号，并通过热扩散机制在卫星轨道网格中传播，进而激励网格节点状态进行伊辛模型演化, 仅通过邻居交互涌现出全局的多智能体协作行为。仿真结果表明，本文提出的伊辛启发合作强化学习方法能有效缓解奖励稀疏性问题，并加速算法收敛。与基线方法相比，本方法在吞吐量和时延方面均表现出更优性能，尤其在高度动态的拓扑场景中效果显著。

**Keywords:** Routing Optimization; Low Earth Orbit (LEO) Satellite Constellations; Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning; Ising Model; Heuristic Algorithm;



To meet the growing demands for low-latency, energy-efficient, and dynamically adaptive routing in low-Earth orbit (LEO) satellite constellation networks, this paper proposes a distributed routing architecture and an Ising-Heuristic Cooperative Reinforcement Learning (IHCRL) framework for optimization. In the proposed approach, agents proactively perceive the environment and make decentralized routing decisions to minimize E2E delay and energy consumption while balancing link loads. Deep neural network(DNN) is used to generate local excitation signals based on the estimated value function, which are then propagated through a diffusion process across the satellite orbital grid. This process stimulates the evolution of node states following the Ising model, enabling the emergence of global multi-agent cooperative behaviors solely through neighbor interactions. Experiment results demonstrate that the proposed IHCRL method effectively mitigates reward sparsity and accelerates convergence. Compared to baseline methods, the proposed approach exhibits superior performance in terms of throughput and latency, particularly in highly dynamic topology scenarios.
