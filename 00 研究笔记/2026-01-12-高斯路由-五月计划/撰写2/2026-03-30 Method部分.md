Due to the optimization problem being a distributed, complex nonlinear programming problem with multiple constraints involving random variables, Reinforcement Learning (RL) is adopted to address this problem. We assume that the LEO satellite constellation routing problem satisfies a Partially Observable Markov Decision Process (POMDP), where each satellite constitutes an agent in a multi-agent environment. The policy is optimized by maximizing the accumulated reward, formulated as:
$$
\pi^* = \arg \max_\pi~ \mathbb{E}[\sum_{t=0}^\infty \gamma^t r_t \mid S_0]
$$
where the reward provides feedback based on the action and the resulting state transition, with rewards $r_t^i \in \mathcal{R}$ defined as:
$$
r_t^i = \left \{
\begin{aligned}
&r_\text{done} - D_\text{prop}(i,j) - \delta_1(\rho_t^i-1) - \delta_3 (|\mathbf{P}_i|- \text{TTL}) \\
&r_\text{next} - [D_\text{prop}(i,j) + D_\text{tx}(i,j) +  D_\text{queue}(j)] 
\\&- \delta_1 \max\{0, \rho^j-1 \}-\delta_2(Q_t^i - Q_\text{max})\\
&r_\text{lost} \\
\end{aligned}
\right.
$$
where the reward is categorized into three constant base values: successful done($r_\text{done}$), forwarding to the next hop($r_\text{next}$), and packet loss($r_\text{lost}$), respectively. $\delta_k$ are penalty coefficient.

整个环境中所有智能体的联合状态称为整体状态, 服从MDP过程, 然而每次观测并不能得到全局状态, 只能得到 $x_t \subset \mathcal{S}$ 称为局部状态, 和历史观测以及动作共同形成长度为 $T$ 的上下文窗口  $X_t^i = \{x_{t-T}^i, a_{t-T}^i, x_{t-T+1}^i,...x_t^i\}$, 定义意图编码器 $f_\omega: \mathcal{X} \rightarrow \mathbb{R}^d$ 将上下文窗口映射为意图向量
$$
u_k^i = f_\omega(X_t^i) = \text{GRU}(h_{k-1}^i, x_{k-1}^i, a_{k-1}^i; \omega)
$$
每个智能体维护自身的意图, 并计算耦合张量
$$
J_{ij} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{d}} u_i^T u_j
$$
在Dueling DQN架构下, 智能体通过瞬时观测做出动作评价, 可以给出状态值函数和动作值函数的分解形式
$$
Q_{\psi, \phi}([x_t^i, u_i], a_t^i) = V_{\psi}([x_t^i, u_i]) + A_{\phi}([x_t^i, u_i], a_t^i) - \max_a A_\phi([x_t^i, u_i], a)
$$
每个智能体维护一个Ising自旋构型 $\sigma_i \in \{+1, -1\}$, 得到在当前观测状态 $x_t^i$ 下的局部场哈密顿量为
$$
H_\text{eff}([x_t^i, u_i]) =  \sum_{j \in \text{Ne}_i}J_{ij}\sigma_j + \xi V_{\psi}([x_t^i, u_i])
$$
构型自旋的翻转按照如下概率进行
$$
P(\uparrow \downarrow \sigma_i) = \min\{1, e^{-2\beta \sigma_i H_\text{eff}([x_t^i, u_i])} \}
$$
按照如下策略进行动作采样, 其中 $\frac{1}{Z_q}$ 为概率归一化系数
$$
A_t^i \sim \pi(A \mid x_t^i, \mathbf{u}, \boldsymbol{\sigma};\Theta) = \frac{1}{Z_q} \exp(Q_{\psi, \phi}([x_t^i, u_i], a_t^i) -2\Theta \sigma_i H_\text{eff}([x_t^i, u_i]))
$$



###### 模型定义

观测空间定义为: The state space for agent $i$ is defined as $x_t^i \in \mathcal{X}$, which includes: the longitude and latitude of satellite $i$, $\mathbf{r}_t^i$, neighboring satellites $\mathbf{r}_t^j, j \in \text{Ne}_i$, the destination gateway $\mathbf{r}^{G}$, all in $[-180^\circ, +180^\circ]^2$; the current queue length and load status of the local node $Q_t^i, \rho_t^i$; the queue length and load status of neighboring nodes $Q_t^j, \rho_t^j; j \in \text{Ne}_i$; the link connectivity to neighbors $C_t^j \in \{0,1\}; j \in \text{Ne}_i$; and the accumulated delay and hop count of the current packet $\tau_t^i, h_t^i$.
$$
x_t^i\in \mathcal{X}  = \{\mathbf{r}_t^{i,\text{Ne},G},  Q_t^{i,\text{Ne}}, \rho_t^{i, \text{Ne}},  C_t^\text{Ne}, \tau_t^i, h_t^i\}
$$




###### 交替训练框架

由于Dueling DQN 可以使用经验回放的独立性以提升样本效率而intent vector的生成必须保留序列性，因此在观测过程中将两种结构的数据分别储存, $(x_t^i, u_i, a_t^i, r_t^i, x_{t+1}^i) \sim \mathcal{D}_q$用于经验回放, 以及 $X_t^i \sim \mathcal{D}_\tau$ 用于意图向量的轨迹学习.

Dueling DQN目标为
$$
\mathcal{L}_q(\psi, \phi) = \mathbb{E}_{(x_t^i, u_i, a_t^i, r_t^i, x_{t+1}^i) \sim \mathcal{D}_q}[(r_t + \gamma \max_a Q_{\psi, \phi}(x_{t+1}^i, a)   - Q_{\psi, \phi}(x_t^i, a_t^i) )]
$$
参数更新为梯度下降, 梯度按常规反向传播穿过 $V_\psi$ 和 $A_\phi$ 两个支流.
$$
(\psi, \phi)_{t+1}^i \leftarrow (\psi, \phi)_{t}^i  + \eta_q \nabla_{(\psi, \phi)} \mathcal{L}_q(\psi, \phi)
$$
在一个episode中, 通过最小化 $\mathcal{L}_q(\psi, \phi) $ 可以进行多次训练, 以得到较为准确的动作值和状态值估计, 随后, 在一个episode结束后, 利用搜集到的轨迹信息进行GRU的更新, 其目标为最大化轨迹回报, $ V_{\psi}([x_t^i, u_i])$ 已经学习到了 $\boldsymbol{u}$ 的回报评估情况, 因此策略梯度表述为:
$$
\nabla_\omega \mathcal{L}_u(\omega) = \mathbb{E}_{X_t^i \sim \mathcal{D}_\tau}[\sum_{t=1}^T\gamma^t\nabla_\omega f_\omega(X_t^i) \nabla_u  V([x_t^i, u])\big|_{u=f_\omega(X_t^i)}]
$$
参数更新为梯度上升
$$
\omega_{t+1}^i \leftarrow \omega_{t}^i  + \eta_\omega \nabla_\omega \mathcal{L}_u(\omega)
$$
